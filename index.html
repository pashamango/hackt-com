what(1) — what this is

The Temple attests builders. Machine side: signed mandates of agents, published where anyone can verify them — DNS TXT as the trust anchor. Human side: residencies and rituals for people who ship.

The Temple serves; it does not own. No membership, no lock-in by design. Every attestation ships as an MIT-licensed instrument anyone can run.
